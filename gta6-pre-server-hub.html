GTA 6 Server Hosting — the quick guide

Official dedicated servers for GTA 6 depend on what Rockstar makes available. Many people are already searching for GTA 6 Server Hosting, Set up a server and community hosting.

  1. Check the status. Is the official infrastructure online? → Status Page
  2. Define your goal. Friends, a clan, or public roleplay? Your choice determines the required slots and performance.
  3. Choose a host. Rent a game server or VPS instead of using a home PC for 24/7 uptime and a stable IP.
  4. Voice alongside it. TeamSpeak or a similar service for your community.
  5. Rules & mods. Use only what the platform and Rockstar allow — no illegal tools.
