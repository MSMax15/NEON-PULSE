Quick Answer: Rent a TeamSpeak Server

A TeamSpeak server is a voice server you manage yourself, with your own channels, permissions, and address. It runs most reliably on gemietetem Hosting with 24/7 uptime, rather than on a home PC.

  1. Plan your slots. Enough for the number of people online at the same time, plus a little spare capacity.
  2. Choose a location. Choose a location close to your players to keep latency low.
  3. Start the server in your control panel. Den Admin-Berechtigungsschlüssel (Privilege Key) sicher speichern.
  4. Create channels and permissions. Zum Beispiel Lobby, Teams, Admin-Kanal.
  5. Share the server address. Set rules and invite your group.

TeamSpeak or Discord?

Discord (kostenlos)

  • No server to manage and no technical setup
  • More than enough for many groups
  • The service and its rules are managed by Discord

TeamSpeak (your own server)

  • Your own address and full control over permissions
  • Unabhängig von Plattform-Änderungen
  • Hosting costs money, but you set the rules

What to Look For

  • Slots: gleichzeitige Nutzer plus Reserve
  • Standort: Latency to your group (choose a nearby region)
  • Panel & Backups: einfache Verwaltung, Sicherungen
  • Schutz: check with the provider whether DDoS protection is included
  • Vertrag: Check the contract term and cancellation policy before buying

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a TeamSpeak server cost?

Pricing depends on slots, rental term, and provider and may change. Always check the current price with the provider.

Wie viele Slots brauche ich?

As a rule of thumb, plan for the number of people online at the same time plus a little spare capacity. More slots can usually be added later.

Brauche ich Technikwissen?

No. Most providers offer a web control panel. Channels and permissions are easy to learn.

Do I need TeamSpeak for GTA 6?

No. A voice server is independent of the game. Before launch, there is no official information about GTA 6 community servers; see our GTA 6 server guide.

Is TeamSpeak or Discord better?

It depends on your needs. Discord is free and simple; TeamSpeak gives you your own server and control over its rules.
