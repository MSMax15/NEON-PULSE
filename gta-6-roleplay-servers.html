GTA 6 Roleplay Servers: Where Things Stand

Roleplay communities run on GTA V today. Here is what that means for GTA 6 and how to prepare now.

Roleplay servers as you know them run on FiveM for GTA V. As far as we know there are no official community servers for GTA 6. Prepare community, rules, and tech now instead of waiting on rumors.

How does GTA RP work today?

Roleplay servers use multiplayer frameworks for GTA V, mainly FiveM. The server runs at a host and players connect with a client. More in our FiveM guide. FiveM’s developer Cfx.re has been part of Rockstar since 2023. To our knowledge Rockstar has not confirmed whether or how this carries over to GTA 6.

What you can prepare now

  • Community: Discord or TeamSpeak, clear rules, an admin team.
  • Know-how: server setup, Lua/JavaScript basics, resource management, see How to set up a FiveM server.
  • Concept: what story, jobs, and rules should your server have?

What to watch out for

Do not trust “GTA 6 server” offers that sell slots already: official community servers are not known as of now. Check sources and providers carefully.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there GTA 6 roleplay servers?

As of now we know of no official community servers for GTA 6. Roleplay currently runs on GTA V with FiveM.

Can I use my FiveM server for GTA 6?

Not known. FiveM is built for GTA V. Watch official announcements.

How do I start now?

With a rented FiveM server and a voice server for your community.
