FiveM Server Hosting

Want your own FiveM server for roleplay or your community? Here is why renting is usually the better choice.

Quick Answer: Renting a FiveM Server

FiveM is a multiplayer modification for GTA V. The most reliable way to run a stable server is a game server host: 24/7 uptime, a fixed IP, and often DDoS protection. Self-hosting works, but is usually impractical for public communities.

  1. Choose a host. Check that FiveM/GTA V servers are offered and the location fits your players.
  2. Pick your size. Match RAM and slots to player count and scripts.
  3. Set up the server. Start it in the web panel, upload resources, configure whitelist or OneSync.
  4. Build your community. Add voice (e.g. TeamSpeak), rules, and admins.

Self-hosting or renting?

Self-hosting

  • Upload speed and stability depend on your internet
  • Your PC must stay on
  • Limited protection against attacks
  • Fine for private tests with 2 to 3 friends

Renting (for communities)

  • 24/7 uptime and a fixed IP
  • Panel, backups, often DDoS protection
  • Scales as your player base grows
  • Less technical work for you as admin

What to look for

  • Player count: Private server, small clan, or public roleplay?
  • Scripts & framework: ESX, QBCore, or custom resources: more load needs more RAM
  • Location: A European data center lowers ping for EU players
  • Voice: In-game voice or a separate TeamSpeak server
  • Rules: Follow the current Cfx/FiveM policies

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FiveM?

FiveM is a multiplayer mod for Grand Theft Auto V that lets communities run their own servers, for example roleplay. FiveM is not GTA 6.

Do I need a legal copy of GTA V?

Yes. FiveM requires a legitimate GTA V installation. See the current Cfx/FiveM policies for details.

How much RAM do I need for a FiveM server?

It depends on player count and scripts. Small private servers need less, public roleplay servers with many resources need considerably more. Follow the provider’s recommendations and leave headroom.

Does this apply to GTA 6?

No. As of now, GTA 6 has no official community servers like FiveM. See our GTA 6 server guide.
