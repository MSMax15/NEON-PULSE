# Launch video setup

1. Open the `launch-video` folder.
2. Copy your MP4 into it.
3. Rename the file to `gta6-launch.mp4`.
4. Keep the folder beside `index.html` when uploading the website.

## Preview
- Open `index.html` locally: a private 30-second preview countdown starts automatically.
- Or open `https://pulseneon.de/?preview=1` on the live site.
- The actual GTA 6 release countdown is unchanged.
- The launch intro can be skipped using **Enter Website**.

The public launch intro remains capped at 8 seconds so visitors are not forced to wait. Preview mode can play for up to 30 seconds or until the video ends.

## Sound and end card
- Visitors choose **Start with Sound** to begin playback with audio. Browsers do not allow websites to force autoplay with sound; this click is required by browser policy.
- The launch title and message appear only after the video ends.
- Visitors can choose Continue muted or Skip intro. The end card then closes automatically after a few seconds, or immediately with Enter Website.
