Status Alert / Formspree

When you subscribe to status alerts, Formspree, Inc. processes your email address and the consent you provide. If the webhook connection is enabled, your email address is transferred through a server-side service at Cloudflare an Buttondown so Buttondown can send you a confirmation email. Your address only becomes an active subscription after you confirm it, and can then receive status updates.

Die Anmeldung kann ohne Bestätigung nicht für Status-E-Mails verwendet werden. Du kannst dich über den Abmeldelink in jeder Status-E-Mail abmelden. For Auskunft oder Löschung kannst du dich über die Kontaktmöglichkeit im Legal Notice melden. Bitte beachte die Privacy Policy der eingebundenen Anbieter: Formspree, Cloudflare und Buttondown.

Affiliate Links

When you click ZAP-Hosting links, ZAP-Hosting’s privacy policy applies. We may receive a commission at no extra cost to you.

Partner banners (ZAP-Hosting)

On several pages, banner images are loaded directly from ZAP-Hosting servers. This means your IP address is transmitted to ZAP-Hosting when the page loads, even if you do not click the banner. After clicking a partner link, ZAP-Hosting may set cookies to attribute the commission. Its privacy policy applies.

Fonts and status display

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google. The status display reads a file from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub), with this website as a fallback. This transmits your IP address to GitHub.
