FiveM After the GTA 6 Launch

Updated: November 7, 2026 · Unofficial fan & affiliate site

Quick take: FiveM remains a GTA V / PC ecosystem. Rockstar/Cfx.re have not confirmed whether or when a comparable option will be available for GTA 6 at console launch. Existing GTA V roleplay servers can continue as long as the game, FiveM, and the host support them.

FiveM Hosting Guide Server Packages

What We Know About Launch

  • GTA 6 launches on consoles — community frameworks such as FiveM have historically been PC-focused.
  • “SixM” is a community term, kein bestätigtes offizielles Produkt.
  • GTA Online / GTA V roleplay will not automatically disappear on the GTA 6 release date.

What Communities Can Do Now

  1. FiveM-Server stabil halten (Backups, Rules, Mods nur erlaubt).
  2. Maintain your voice setup and team structure.
  3. Know your budget and hosting tier — see Pakete.
  4. GTA‑6-Status beobachten: Status Page.
