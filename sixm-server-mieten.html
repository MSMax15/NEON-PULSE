Looking to rent a SixM-Rent a Server for a community or roleplay? This guide explains what SixM refers to, why renting is often more practical than using a home PC, and how to get started.

Klartext: “SixM” is a community term, not a live product confirmed by Rockstar. This page explains hosting options and links to partner offers where relevant.

Kurzantwort: SixM Server Hosting

SixM refers to the idea of community multiplayer around GTA 6, comparable to FiveM for GTA V: private servers, custom rules, and often roleplay. Whether and how features are officially supported depends on the community and providers. For stable public servers, the usual principles still apply:

  1. Rent hosting with 24/7 uptime, a fixed IP, and enough RAM/player slots.
  2. Choose a location (choose a region close to your players for low latency).
  3. Use the control panel — start the server, upload resources, and configure backups.
  4. Voice alongside it — TeamSpeak or in-game voice, depending on your setup.
  5. Rules and compliance — use only permitted mods/tools and follow the platform and game terms.

SixM refers to the idea of community multiplayer servers in the GTA 6 ecosystem, similar to the concept behind FiveM for GTA V. Technical details, clients, and permitted features depend on the platform and its current rules. Always check the official sources of the relevant mod/server platform.
