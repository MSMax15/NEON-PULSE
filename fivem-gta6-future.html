FiveM and GTA 6

Will FiveM disappear at GTA 6 launch? Here is a calm, factual read for communities.

Short answer: No confirmed public statement means “FiveM dies the day GTA 6 launches.” Plans can change; treat rumors as rumors. FiveM is built around GTA V, not a finished official GTA 6 multiplayer product.

Why this question trends

Players worry that a new GTA 6 online era will kill GTA V roleplay platforms. Interest is valid. Certainty from random posts is not.

What we can say clearly

  • FiveM is a GTA V multiplayer modification platform.
  • GTA 6 official multiplayer will follow Rockstar’s own design and timeline.
  • Rockstar’s relationship with Cfx.re / creator tooling has been discussed in industry coverage — still not a substitute for official launch docs.
  • Communities should plan for both: enjoy V platforms today, watch official GTA 6 online news for tomorrow.

Practical takeaway

Do not panic-buy “GTA 6 FiveM servers.” Do use solid hosting and voice if you run a V community now. Re-read guides after official PC/online details land.
