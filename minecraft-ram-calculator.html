Minecraft RAM Calculator

Pick your server type and player count to get a rule-of-thumb RAM figure.

Small servers without mods often run on 2 to 4 GB, modpacks need considerably more. Try the calculator; your modpack’s recommendation always comes first.

Suggested: – GB RAM

Rule of thumb, not a guarantee. Your modpack’s recommendation comes first.

How we calculate

  • Vanilla and plugins start at about 2 GB.
  • Mods start at about 4 GB.
  • Each player adds some RAM (more with mods).
  • Large modpacks get a surcharge.

More RAM is not always better

Too much RAM can slow garbage collection. A good CPU and the right Java version matter more. See Rent a Minecraft Server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the calculator?

It gives a rough rule of thumb, not a measurement. Real load depends on world, redstone, and mods.

Is 2 GB enough?

For a small vanilla server with few players, often yes.

Does Bedrock need less?

Bedrock servers are usually lighter. Check the provider’s specs.
