Rent a Server or Self-Host?

Updated: November 7, 2026 · Unofficial fan & affiliate site

Quick take: For testing, your own PC is often enough. For a 24/7 community, roleplay, and friends who need to join anytime, rented hosting is usually the easier and more reliable option.

View the 3 plans GTA 6 Hosting Guide

Self-Hosting

  • Low-cost way to learn
  • Full control over your hardware
  • Upload speed, electricity, IP configuration, and household availability can all be limitations
  • Your PC has to stay on — impractical for public servers

Rent a Server

  • 24/7 uptime and a fixed IP
  • Panel, Backups, oft DDoS-Schutz
  • Scales as your community grows
  • Monatliche Kosten statt Eigenbau-Stress

Which Option Is Right for You?

  1. Just testing / 2–3 friends occasionally: self-hosting may be enough.
  2. Clan, roleplay, or public server: rent a server.
  3. Voice alongside it: Rent a TeamSpeak Server or in-game voice.
