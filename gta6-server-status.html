What Do the Status Labels Mean?

  • Online / Up: Services are reachable — local issues (internet, console, account) may still occur.
  • Eingeschränkt / Limited: Partial outages, queues, or individual features may be affected.
  • Down / Gestört: Major outage — often affecting many players at once.
  • maintenance: Planned or unplanned maintenance by Rockstar.
  • Not listed yet: Before launch, or if Rockstar has not yet listed GTA 6 on its status page.

GTA 6 Won’t Connect — What Should You Do?

  1. Check Rockstar Status. If an outage is reported there, local troubleshooting is unlikely to help.
  2. Network: Restart your router, test wired/Wi-Fi connections, and turn off your VPN.
  3. Platform: Check PS5 / Xbox network status and game updates.
  4. Account: Check your Rockstar / console login and possible launch queues.
  5. Try Again Later: Launch days can be busy — be patient and follow official channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTA 6 online?

That depends on the launch and Rockstar status. Before the November 19, 2026 release, online services are not live yet. After launch, always check the official status page and the status indicator at the top of this page.

Are GTA 6 servers really down?

Many social media reports are speculation. Rely on Rockstar Service Status and, when available, our status indicator. A single connection error does not mean there is a global outage.
