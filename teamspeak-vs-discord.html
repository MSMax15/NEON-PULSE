TeamSpeak vs Discord

Both work. The question is what you need.

Discord is enough for most small groups: free, easy, many features. TeamSpeak pays off if you want your own address, fine-grained permissions, and full control of the server.

The comparison

DiscordTeamSpeak
CostFree, optional subscriptionsYour own server costs rent
SetupReady right awayRent and set up a server
ControlPlatform sets the rulesYou set address and permissions
FeaturesText, video, bots, streamingFocus on voice, fine permissions
DataHeld by DiscordHeld on your server

Which one when?

  • Small friend group: Discord.
  • Large clan or roleplay with permission structure: TeamSpeak.
  • Both: Discord for text, TeamSpeak for voice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TeamSpeak better than Discord?

Not in general. It depends on whether you prioritize control or simplicity.

How much does TeamSpeak cost?

Your own server costs rent at the provider, and the price changes.

Can I use both?

Yes, many communities combine both.
