Quick Answer: Renting a FiveM Server

FiveM is a multiplayer mod framework for GTA V. The most reliable way to run a stable server is through gemietetes Game-Server Hosting — with 24/7 uptime, a fixed IP, and often DDoS protection. Self-hosting on a home PC is possible, but usually impractical for public communities.

  1. Choose a host. Anbieter mit FiveM-/GTA-Unterstützung und Standort nah an deinen Spielern (z. B. Europe).
  2. Choose your plan size. Match RAM and player slots to your expected player count and scripts.
  3. Set up your server. Launch through the web panel, upload resources/scripts, and configure a whitelist or OneSync if needed.
  4. Bring your community together. Optionally run TeamSpeak/voice alongside it; establish rules and assign admins.

Why Rent FiveM Hosting?

Self-Hosting

  • Upload speed and stability depend on your internet connection
  • Your PC must stay on — using electricity and making noise
  • Limited protection against attacks
  • Fine for private tests with 2–3 friends

Renting (for communities)

  • 24/7 uptime and a fixed IP
  • Panel, Backups, oft DDoS-Schutz
  • Scales as your player base grows
  • Less technical work for you as an admin

What to Decide Before You Start

  • Spielerzahl: Private server, small clan, or public roleplay?
  • Scripts & Framework: ESX, QBCore, or custom resources — more load requires more RAM
  • Standort: Latency to your audience (choose a nearby region)
  • Voice: In-game voice or a separate TeamSpeak server
  • Rechtliches: Follow FiveM/Cfx rules and server policies

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FiveM?

FiveM ist ein Multiplayer-Mod für Grand Theft Auto V, mit dem Communities eigene Server (z. B. Roleplay) betreiben können. Es ist nicht identisch mit dem kommenden GTA 6 – aber die Suchintention „eigener GTA-Server“ landet oft hier.

Brauche ich GTA V legal?

Ja. FiveM setzt eine legale GTA-V-Installation voraus. Details stehen in den aktuellen Cfx/FiveM-Richtlinien.

Wie viel RAM brauche ich?

Requirements depend on player count and scripts. Small private servers can start with less, while public roleplay servers with many resources need considerably more. Check the provider’s recommendations and leave some headroom.

Does this apply to GTA 6?

As of this pre-launch guide, GTA 6 has no approved community dedicated servers like FiveM. FiveM remains the current option for GTA V communities. For GTA 6 launch information, see our GTA 6 Server Guide.
